Eric Whitacre Choir. The nine signals named in Spitfire's article are offered: Close, Tree, Ambient, Outriggers (group Core); Mid, Gallery (group Auxiliary); Big, Medium, Small (group Stereo). The other codes (CL02-CL05, ST04) are not offered and are always copied, because Spitfire says they are required for large sections of the library.

After removing mic positions the plugin may show Error 4 (missing samples). Turn on the plugin setting "disable missing samples" (Spitfire's own guidance for trimmed libraries) to load without it.
